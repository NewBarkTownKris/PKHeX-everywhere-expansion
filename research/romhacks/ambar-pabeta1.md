# Pokémon Ambar pabeta1 — experimental read-only recovery

The supplied Ruby-derived ROM has SHA-256 `ad5c98cea663e6eb83d0967fb301589d7b48f4087fef30bb143c0f77c16c6443` and mGBA CRC `52FF506E`. The local reader exports qualifying real party/PC Pokémon from explicitly selected Ruby-layout battery saves and exact-build mGBA version 7 states. Other revisions, old VBA states and save editing are not supported by this profile. Shared Ruby headers cannot identify a hack; select the matching revision.

A Gary Edition Eevee was inserted into a copy of a game-created Ambar Ruby-layout save, then opened in Pokémon Ambar pabeta1, displayed in its party screen, re-saved in mGBA and reopened. Its battery and mGBA native party bytes and PK6 species, nickname, moves, OT and ID matched. This is a **cross-game synthetic seed**, not an independently caught Ambar pabeta1 specimen. PC records are synthetic tests only, and real boxes, other species, forms and changed mechanics are not yet validated.

The ROM species-name table at `0x1F716C` yielded 359 official-name/original-Gen-3-ID candidates. Candidate move and item tables yielded 295 and 175 exact-name matches. A name match does not establish effects, abilities, forms or growth. Unknown or custom species are skipped; eggs and unsupported move, item or ball combinations are retained only as native records where applicable. PK6 conversion assumes standard ability and PID-derived shiny, accepts the standard Poké Ball and leaves location unknown. Native records and original source metadata are retained; no retail encounter legality is implied.

`HistoricGen3.InspectRubySave` validates the 14-sector generation/checksums before interpreting the Ruby layout. For this exact ROM, mGBA live party count is at IWRAM `0x03004350`, party at `0x03004360`, trainer data at EWRAM `0x02024EA4`, and PC storage at `0x020300A0`. Party count/bytes were checked against the game re-save and storage bytes against the same save; a synthetic occupied PC slot checks indexing. The supplied PokeDaisy 1.3.2 source's `scripts/port_field_items.py` and `RetailPortsGen.kt` were useful prior references for retail Ruby party and trainer addresses; the actual Ambar pabeta1 state was checked independently. No PokeDaisy implementation was copied. [Profile checks](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs) exercise synthetic inputs. Browser upload/download remains unverified. Private ROMs, saves, states and records are excluded from this repository.

Move names are Spanish. There are 294 official English/Spanish exact-name matches plus native ID 39 `LÁTIGO`, whose in-game summary describes the standard Tail Whip effect. Source item names yielded 175 conservative matches. Custom and unmatched species are skipped; ID 280 is outside this revision’s allowlist. The similarly named Ambar Beta 1 is a separately fingerprinted profile.

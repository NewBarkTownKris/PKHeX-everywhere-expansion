# Pokémon Gary Edition Beta 1 — local compatibility

The supplied FireRed-based ROM (SHA-256 prefix `6075cbb388bb`, mGBA CRC `912918D8`) has experimental read-only PK6 export from explicitly selected in-game battery saves and exact-build mGBA states. Generic FireRed headers cannot identify this revision; users must choose the matching source. Other revisions and VBA states are not validated.

A fresh in-game Eevee was obtained from Oak's Pallet Town lab, saved normally and reloaded. The battery and mGBA state readers recovered identical starter identities and source PID. Resulting PK6 files reopened with matching species, trainer and moves. Its native met ID 88 was observed in Pallet Town and the ROM's own location table names ID 88 `PALLET TOWN`. For that ID only, PK6 uses an HG origin and the **Kanto region** label as a regional approximation; original origin and ID remain in metadata and the native record. All other met IDs remain unknown. Occupied-PC testing is synthetic only, not gameplay-derived.

The candidate source species table has 386 exact official Gen 3 name/original-ID matches. Move and item tables have 350 and 228 standard-name matches respectively. Name matches are not proof of unmodified stats, abilities or mechanics. The exporter skips unmatched species, refuses eggs and unsupported moves/items, accepts only the checked ordinary Poké Ball, and assumes standard ability/PID-derived shiny semantics. Boxed level uses standard growth data. The raw native record and metadata preserve source values; no claim of retail legality is made. Code: `Historic/VbaParty.cs`, `Historic/VbaPortable.cs`; checks: `ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs`. ROMs and saved games are not included in the repository.

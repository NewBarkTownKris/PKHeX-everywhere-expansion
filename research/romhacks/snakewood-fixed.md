# Pokémon Snakewood Fixed — experimental read-only recovery

The supplied ROM has SHA-256 `c2dab6e9348a09c5b74504b43e6cc556c0a10f5556969017662e1e97dbc5cacb` and mGBA CRC `07001014`. This local, revision-specific reader exports recognized real party/PC Pokémon from a selected Ruby-layout battery save or a CRC-identified mGBA version 7 state. A shared FireRed header cannot identify a particular hack: users must choose the correct revision for battery saves. VBA state support and save editing are not implemented for this profile.

A Eevee fixture was **seeded from a different hack**, then displayed by this ROM's party screen, re-saved in mGBA and reopened. Battery and mGBA native party bytes, nickname, OT, ID and portable PK6 moves/checksum agreed. This is evidence for a single cross-game test record, not a Pokémon caught or obtained in this game. PC records and pointer bounds have synthetic tests only; real occupied boxes, independently obtained Pokémon and additional species, forms, mechanics and ball/shiny behavior remain unverified.

The exact ROM's candidate species table at `0x1F716C` yielded 272 official-name/original-Gen-3-ID matches. Its candidate move/item tables yielded 330 and 150 exact-name matches. These comparisons identify candidates, not identical in-game effects, forms or ability behavior. Unknown/custom species are skipped; eggs and unsupported move/item/ball combinations are not converted. PK6 uses the candidate tables, standard ability/PID-derived shiny assumptions, only the standard Poké Ball, and an unknown met location. Native bytes, OT, PID and original met information are retained in metadata. No retail legality is implied.

Implementation: `Historic/VbaParty.cs`, `Historic/vba-snakewood-fixed*.json`, and [synthetic profile checks](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs). Player party is at IWRAM `0x03004360`, party count at `0x03004350`, trainer at EWRAM `0x02024EA4` and PC at `0x020300A0`. Browser upload and download have not been tested end-to-end. Private ROMs, saves, states and extracted records remain outside this repository.

This game changes some species names and contains custom designs. Only exact standard-name/original-ID candidates are eligible; other species are skipped, and candidate identity alone does not prove unchanged game mechanics. The fixture was seeded from another game; real occupied PC data and independent catches have not been tested.

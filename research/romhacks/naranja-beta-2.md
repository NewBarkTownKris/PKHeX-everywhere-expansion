# Pokémon Naranja Beta 2 — experimental read-only recovery

The supplied Ruby-derived ROM has SHA-256 `e530533ea50c779dd5e246e9115d318ab5f16289188aaf740c8c6befdf972322` and mGBA CRC `E4C181BF`. The local reader exports qualifying real party/PC Pokémon from explicitly selected Ruby-layout battery saves and exact-build mGBA version 7 states. Other revisions, old VBA states and save editing are not supported by this profile. Shared Ruby headers cannot identify a hack; select the matching revision.

A Gary Edition Eevee was inserted into a copy of a game-created Ambar Ruby-layout save, then opened in Pokémon Naranja Beta 2, displayed in its party screen, re-saved in mGBA and reopened. Its battery and mGBA native party bytes and PK6 species, nickname, moves, OT and ID matched. This is a **cross-game synthetic seed**, not an independently caught Pokémon Naranja Beta 2 specimen. PC records are synthetic tests only, and real boxes, other species, forms and changed mechanics are not yet validated.

The ROM species-name table at `0x1F716C` yielded 380 official-name/original-Gen-3-ID candidates. Candidate move and item tables yielded 349 and 159 exact-name matches. A name match does not establish effects, abilities, forms or growth. Unknown or custom species are skipped; eggs and unsupported move, item or ball combinations are retained only as native records where applicable. PK6 conversion assumes standard ability and PID-derived shiny, accepts the standard Poké Ball and leaves location unknown. Native records and original source metadata are retained; no retail encounter legality is implied.

`HistoricGen3.InspectRubySave` validates the 14-sector generation/checksums before interpreting the Ruby layout. For this exact ROM, mGBA live party count is at IWRAM `0x03004350`, party at `0x03004360`, trainer data at EWRAM `0x02024EA4`, and PC storage at `0x020300A0`. Party count/bytes were checked against the game re-save and storage bytes against the same save; a synthetic occupied PC slot checks indexing. The supplied PokeDaisy 1.3.2 source's `scripts/port_field_items.py` and `RetailPortsGen.kt` were useful prior references for retail Ruby party and trainer addresses; the actual Pokémon Naranja Beta 2 state was checked independently. No PokeDaisy implementation was copied. [Profile checks](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs) exercise synthetic inputs. Browser upload/download remains unverified. Private ROMs, saves, states and records are excluded from this repository.

The supplied Naranja revision has Spanish interface text, but the seeded Eevee summary displayed the English names TACKLE, TAIL WHIP and HELPING HAND, matching this ROM’s candidate move table for those sampled moves. Other move behavior is not established by that sample. The separately fingerprinted other Naranja revision uses a separate profile.

This revision’s item table uses mixed-case Spanish labels; the earlier uppercase anchor search missed it. The examined table at `0x3C5564` produced 159 conservative exact-name item matches, so unmatched held items are not converted. The source moves at `0x1F8320` produced 349 matches.

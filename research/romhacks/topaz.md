# Pokémon Topaz — experimental read-only recovery

The supplied Ruby-derived ROM has SHA-256 `528011e6a51ff41142035642d9750bc175494b64f614fd9958673aa6498c0f7b` and mGBA CRC `1B921419`. The local reader exports qualifying real party/PC Pokémon from explicitly selected Ruby-layout battery saves and exact-build mGBA version 7 states. Other revisions, old VBA states and save editing are not supported by this profile. Shared Ruby headers cannot identify a hack; select the matching revision.

A Gary Edition Eevee was inserted into a copy of a game-created Ambar Ruby-layout save, then opened in Topaz, displayed in its party screen, re-saved in mGBA and reopened. Its battery and mGBA native party bytes and PK6 species, nickname, moves, OT and ID matched. This is a **cross-game synthetic seed**, not an independently caught Topaz specimen. PC records are synthetic tests only, and real boxes, other species, forms and changed mechanics are not yet validated.

The ROM species-name table at `0x1F716C` yielded 183 official-name/original-Gen-3-ID candidates. Candidate move and item tables yielded 87 exact-name move candidates and 225 exact-name item candidates. A name match does not establish effects, abilities, forms or growth. Unknown or custom species are skipped; eggs and unsupported move, item or ball combinations are retained only as native records where applicable. PK6 conversion assumes standard ability and PID-derived shiny, accepts the standard Poké Ball and leaves location unknown. Native records and original source metadata are retained; no retail encounter legality is implied.

`HistoricGen3.InspectRubySave` validates the 14-sector generation/checksums before interpreting the Ruby layout. For this exact ROM, mGBA live party count is at IWRAM `0x03004350`, party at `0x03004360`, trainer data at EWRAM `0x02024EA4`, and PC storage at `0x020300A0`. Party count/bytes were checked against the game re-save and storage bytes against the same save; a synthetic occupied PC slot checks indexing. The supplied PokeDaisy 1.3.2 source's `scripts/port_field_items.py` and `RetailPortsGen.kt` were useful prior references for retail Ruby party and trainer addresses; the actual Topaz state was checked independently. No PokeDaisy implementation was copied. [Profile checks](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs) exercise synthetic inputs. Browser upload/download remains unverified. Private ROMs, saves, states and records are excluded from this repository.

Topaz renames many moves. In its own seeded Eevee summary, native move 33 `LUNGE` described a full-body tackle (power 35, accuracy 95), native 39 `SWEET SMILE` described lowering Defense, and native 270 `ASSISTANCE` described boosting the recipient’s moves. These three IDs are conservatively mapped to Tackle, Tail Whip and Helping Hand based on their in-game descriptions; no battle-mechanics equivalence is claimed. In particular, Topaz `LUNGE` is not mapped to the later official move named Lunge. Other renamed moves remain native-only.

# Pokémon Lightning Yellow Demo — local compatibility

The exact supplied FireRed-based ROM (SHA-256 prefix `cd5de0feef16`, mGBA CRC `82F643BF`) has experimental read-only PK6 export from selected in-game battery saves and exact-build mGBA states. FireRed save headers do not uniquely identify hacks; users must choose the right source. Other revisions and VBA state versions are not supported.

A new game obtained a level 5 Pikachu in Professor Oak's Pallet Town laboratory, completed the opening battle, saved and reloaded. The battery and live-state readers produced a checksum-valid PK6 with matching species, nickname, original trainer and moves. The source native met ID 88 is labeled `PALLET TOWN` in the ROM's map-name table, independently matching the visible in-game location. For ID 88 only, the portable PK6 deliberately uses HeartGold origin and **the Kanto region** as a regional approximation; the original source origin and ID remain in metadata and the native record. Other locations remain unknown. Synthetic checks cover PC placement, filters and invalid states; no occupied PC from actual gameplay has been tested.

The ROM species table has 386 exact official Gen 3 name/original-ID matches. Its move/item tables have 350/354 and 228 same-name matches respectively. Matching names alone do not guarantee original mechanics, forms, ability or shiny rules; the exporter uses standard ability and PID shiny assumptions. Unmatched species are skipped, unknown moves/items and eggs are refused, only the ordinary Poké Ball is accepted, and stored PC levels use standard growth. The native record and original metadata are retained and no claim of retail encounter legality is made. Code: `Historic/VbaParty.cs`, `Historic/VbaPortable.cs`; regression tests: `ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs`. No user ROMs or saved game data are distributed here.

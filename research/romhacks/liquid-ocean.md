# Pokémon Liquid Ocean Beta 1 — experimental read-only recovery

**Earlier status:** local VBA party-only recovery before the battery and mGBA additions described below. The supplied Emerald-derived Beta 1 VBA 8 state contains one checksum-valid player party record and a separate opponent. The Alpha 1.2 ROM is a different revision with a different header and is not included by this profile.

ROM SHA-256 fingerprint(s): `cec63f4465c5668262edcd185da2663e7fd6aec8cba1897a016cdf09144b0462`. Source species name tables and their conservative derived allowlists: [liquid-ocean-beta-1](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.RomHacks/Historic/vba-liquid-ocean-beta-1.json). The allowlist requires the same official name and original Generation III internal species ID. Unmatched IDs, including custom species, are skipped. Name/ID agreement is necessary evidence, not proof of unchanged stats, forms, abilities or evolution semantics; the ROM header in a state cannot establish the exact matching ROM.

The [VBA reader](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.RomHacks/Historic/VbaParty.cs) validates version, length, game header, party count and Gen 3 record checksums. Its native `.bin` records retain the original OT, ball, shiny-related PID/OT, moves and met fields; these fields are retained in metadata; eligible records can additionally export to PK6 with an unknown met location and standard ability/shiny assumptions. Species, moves and items use conservative name matches, not proof of matching mechanics. Only standard Poké Balls are presently accepted for portable export. Game-specific ball, shiny, item, move and location behavior has not been verified. Do not assign standard routes or portable origins merely from numeric ID. The party must be independently checked against real gameplay and future tests must include boxed Pokémon and more species.

See [VBA state research](../formats/vba-sgm.md) and [the synthetic checks](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs). Private supplied states are not in this repository.

## Local battery and mGBA expansion (2026-10-29)

For the supplied Emerald-based Beta 1 ROM (`cec63f4465c5…`, CRC `B82CE606`), a fresh player completed the intro, met the rival, received an ordinary Torchic after the professor rescue, saved in game and reopened the copied save. `HistoricGen3.InspectEmeraldSave` validates the 14-sector save before reading party/PC; `VbaParty` uses this revision's source-name species/move/item candidate tables and conditional PK6 conversion. The actual game-created Torchic's trainer, nickname, PID, moves and entire native party record match the reloaded exact-build mGBA state; its PK6 passed identity and checksum checks. PC battery and mGBA records have synthetic coverage only, not a game-created occupied box.

This revision's mGBA version 7 party count is at EWRAM `0x020244E9`, party starts `0x020244EC`, trainer save-block pointer is at IWRAM `0x03005D90`, and PC storage pointer is at `0x03005D94`. Pointer bounds and box indexing are validated. Exact ROM CRC identifies mGBA states; an explicit revision selection is required for the shared-header battery and old VBA 8 paths. Name matches do not prove all changed mechanics, abilities or forms. Only source-name/original-ID real species and supported moves/items are included, only the standard Poké Ball is accepted for portable conversion, shiny and ability use standard assumptions, and location is unknown. Native bytes and original metadata are retained; no save editing is claimed. Local synthetic tests cover Emerald party/PC and mGBA pointer selection; browser chooser and download remain unverified.

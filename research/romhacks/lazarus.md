# Pokémon Lazarus 2.0 — investigation

The supplied ROM has SHA-256 `d756b2783dede0bb2c534d1f07713bef733cf53500388e3912f14220a7e16e97`, mGBA CRC `558AE42F`. Both supplied 128 KiB saves loaded in private mGBA copies with that ROM. Upstream generic save detection labels them `SAV3RS` and reports valid checksums, but that label does not prove Pokémon structures or mappings are correct for this Emerald-expansion hack. Generic no-op output was byte-identical, while the expansion 1.17.1 profile correctly rejected the save.

The nearby PokeDaisy source identifies Lazarus 2.0 as based on expansion 1.10.0 and describes game-specific species, move, item and map tables (`scripts/gen_expansion_tables.py`). Its regional species roster omits entries; national-name table matches alone do not prove all forms, abilities or mechanics. This fork has not yet derived and validated the correct save layout or safe real-species-only export. No portable Pokémon export or in-game edit compatibility is claimed. The original saves were not modified.

A further read-only comparison used the locally documented PokeDaisy live party address `0x0201B960` and count `0x0201B95D` against the supplied `.srm` and its mGBA state. All six 100-byte live party records matched the original save byte-for-byte starting at file offset `0x1238`; all six passed the classic encrypted-record checksum. Their source species IDs include values above 1025, so Gen 3 `PK3.Species` interpretation alone is unsafe. This narrows the next investigation: independently map native IDs and forms from this exact ROM, validate moves/items/balls/shininess and PC storage, and only export known real forms. Compare the second save before implementation.

An independent audit of the exact ROM’s species table (`0xC7A438`, stride `0xD4`, name at entry 1) found 408 case-insensitive official English name/national-ID matches among IDs 1–1025. Four of the six live party IDs are in that conservative candidate set; two exceed 1025 and require explicit form mapping or must be skipped. Names and IDs alone do not validate moves, abilities or forms.

## Experimental recovery implemented

The local next build provides explicitly selected battery recovery and exact-CRC mGBA party/PC recovery to PK6 or PK9. Both supplied saves were tested against states from the supplied ROM: all 268 and 29 occupied native party/box records respectively matched byte-for-byte. For the larger sample, 194 records produced portable files, 23 retained native records with mapping errors, and 51 unsupported species/forms were skipped. For the second, 24 portable files were produced, two retained with errors, and three skipped. Reopened PK6/PK9 checksums, species and trainer IDs, and collection archives were checked. These results do not guarantee every game mechanic or every supported species.

Only the 408 exact English-name/national-ID candidates in the source ROM’s species table are eligible, with forms beyond national IDs excluded. Move IDs map by unique exact English name from the ROM’s move table, not assumed numeric equality; unmatched moves are retained only in native records. PK9 output is used where PK6 does not fit, but only if verified personal data exists for that species. Ability slot, gender and shiny use standard assumptions; held items are removed and balls replaced with Poké Balls. Native record bytes and source IDs are retained, met locations are unknown, and party experience may be adjusted to preserve displayed level. Box level and hack-altered mechanics may differ. No retail legality is implied. The battery reader requires a complete checksum-valid 14-sector slot; only the exact ROM CRC is accepted for mGBA states.

The source name-table and RAM-address investigations were informed by local PokeDaisy `scripts/gen_expansion_tables.py` and `NativeReader.kt`; source files and ROM assets are not copied. Implementation is `Historic/LazarusRecovery.cs`, its generated candidate mapping JSON, and `StateExports`.

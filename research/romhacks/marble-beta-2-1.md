# Pokémon Marble Beta 2.1 — experimental read-only recovery

The supplied ROM has SHA-256 `9cee2fbf92b8dc73ce602a2e8878e21e1570434559927f7d1043a694353aa2a8` and mGBA CRC `2BED9BD8`. This local, revision-specific reader exports recognized real party/PC Pokémon from a selected FireRed-layout battery save or a CRC-identified mGBA version 7 state. A shared FireRed header cannot identify a particular hack: users must choose the correct revision for battery saves. VBA state support and save editing are not implemented for this profile.

A Cyndaquil fixture was **seeded from a different hack**, then displayed by this ROM's party screen, re-saved in mGBA and reopened. Battery and mGBA native party bytes, nickname, OT, ID and portable PK6 moves/checksum agreed. This is evidence for a single cross-game test record, not a Pokémon caught or obtained in this game. PC records and pointer bounds have synthetic tests only; real occupied boxes, independently obtained Pokémon and additional species, forms, mechanics and ball/shiny behavior remain unverified.

The exact ROM's candidate species table at `2383584` yielded 235 official-name/original-Gen-3-ID matches. Its candidate move/item tables yielded 349 and 221 exact-name matches. These comparisons identify candidates, not identical in-game effects, forms or ability behavior. Unknown/custom species are skipped; eggs and unsupported move/item/ball combinations are not converted. PK6 uses the candidate tables, standard ability/PID-derived shiny assumptions, only the standard Poké Ball, and an unknown met location. Native bytes, OT, PID and original met information are retained in metadata. No retail legality is implied.

Implementation: `Historic/VbaParty.cs`, `Historic/vba-marble-beta-2-1*.json`, and [synthetic profile checks](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs). Player party is at EWRAM `0x02024284`, party count at `0x02024029`, trainer and PC pointers at IWRAM `0x0300500C` and `0x03005010`. Browser upload and download have not been tested end-to-end. Private ROMs, saves, states and extracted records remain outside this repository.

The game’s three selectable starters are custom species CHAPEBAYA, COALCHU and TODAD at native IDs 1, 4 and 7. The real-species filter excludes all three instead of misidentifying them as standard starters. A synthetic save check confirms that a custom ID is skipped while a qualifying real boxed Cyndaquil is retained. The 235 source-name/original-ID matches are candidate real Pokémon only; other IDs are excluded, not assumed to be official.

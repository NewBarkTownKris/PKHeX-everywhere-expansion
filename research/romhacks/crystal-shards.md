# Pokémon Crystal Shards Beta 1 — experimental read-only recovery

The exact supplied Emerald-derived ROM has SHA-256 `920443d59fad59ecfcb30c0bf02767e3b8d4ce8b8531d9cf7839202129c8634b` and mGBA ROM CRC `A3938FD1`. The local app can export recognized real party and PC records from explicitly selected complete in-game saves and automatically identified mGBA version 7 states. It does not claim support for other revisions, old VBA states or editing the game save. Shared ROM headers cannot uniquely identify a hack; the user selects the correct game for a battery save.

A fresh mGBA playthrough obtained a standard Cyndaquil from Professor Elm in New Bark Town, saved in game and reopened the save. The battery and mGBA live party bytes matched exactly, and the PK6 checksum, species, nickname, OT, ID and Tackle/Leer moves were checked against the native record. The native met-location field was 0 even for this observed New Bark starter, so location remains unknown rather than treating that number as proof of a place. Box handling was tested using synthetic records, not an in-game occupied PC.

The source-name table yielded 386 candidates with both official Gen 3 species name and matching internal ID. Move and item tables yielded 350 and 223 exact-name candidates, respectively; these are candidate mappings and do not prove unchanged effects, form semantics, abilities or growth. Unknown or custom species are skipped, eggs are refused, and unsupported move, item or ball mappings retain only native records. Portable PK6 assumes standard ability and PID-derived shiny behavior; only the standard Poké Ball is accepted, and met location is unknown. Original native bytes, trainer, PID and location values are preserved in metadata. No retail encounter legality is implied.

`HistoricGen3.InspectEmeraldSave` validates the complete 14-sector generation and checksums. `VbaParty.InspectMgba` uses the Emerald party count `0x020244E9`, party `0x020244EC`, trainer pointer `0x03005D90` and PC pointer `0x03005D94`, with bounds checks. Source candidate resources are `Historic/vba-crystal-shards-beta-1*.json`; [synthetic checks](../../src/PKHeX.Everywhere.ProfileChecks/VbaChecks.cs) cover PC slots and unknown inputs. Browser chooser/download testing remains outstanding. No original ROM, personal save, state or extracted records are committed here.
